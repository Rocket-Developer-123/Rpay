Questions, answered

Still curious? Everything on RPAY is simulated, so the quickest way to find out is to open the app and try a payment.

Launch App
Is real money involved?
No. RPAY is a 100% simulated sandbox ecosystem. Kinex (K) has no cash value, nothing can be deposited or withdrawn, and no bank account, card or real UPI ID is ever linked. It's a safe place to learn, test and demo digital payments.
What is Kinex (K)?
Kinex is the native transactional asset of the RPAY network: the unit every transfer, checkout and receipt is counted in. New accounts start with K 10,000, and balances are kept in a deterministic PostgreSQL ledger, so each balance always equals the sum of its transactions.
Can businesses or shops integrate with RPAY?
Yes. The dynamic QR POS generator lets a shop create a QR code with the bill total already filled in. Customers scan it, confirm the amount and authorize with their PIN. The codes follow the standard upi://pay?pa=…&am=… format, so they work at a physical counter or a virtual checkout.
How do accounts get frozen?
RPAY includes operator-level fraud controls. When activity looks suspicious, an operator can freeze the account while the case is investigated, and payments from that account are blocked until the review is done. The freeze is part of the simulation, so no real funds are ever at stake.
Does it require an app download?
No. RPAY is a mobile-optimized progressive web experience. Open it in your browser and, if you like, install it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like feel.
